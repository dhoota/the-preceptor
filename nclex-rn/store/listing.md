### Description (4000)
Prepare for the NCLEX-RN with original items in every Next Generation format.

Every item type
Multiple choice, select all that apply, select N, drag and drop, drop-down cloze, highlight, matrix, bow-tie and trend items. Each is scored with the rule the NCLEX uses for that type: 0/1, plus/minus or rationale scoring. You see the points you earned and why.

Case studies
Six items per case that walk the clinical judgment steps in order: recognize cues, analyze cues, prioritize hypotheses, generate solutions, take action and evaluate outcomes. The chart unfolds as the case goes on.

Mapped to the 2026 test plan
The bank follows the Client Needs areas of the NCLEX-RN test plan effective April 2026. Practise by area, by item type or by clinical judgment step. Progress shows your points in each area and each step.

An adaptive mock
The mock follows the published exam structure: 85 to 150 items in 5 hours, 3 case studies, and unscored items mixed in. A simple adaptive model picks each item and applies the three stopping rules. At the end you get an honest readiness band: above, near or below the passing standard. Item difficulty is rated by our writers, not measured on real candidates. So the band is a rough guide, never a chance of passing.

Rationales you can trust
Every choice has its own reason, right or wrong. Every item cites a named source. Every calculation is checked by our tests. Items note where practice in Canada differs.

Private and offline
No account. No tracking. Everything stays on your device and works with no signal.

Every item is original. None is taken from NCSBN material or any real exam. Content is written for education and is being reviewed by a nurse educator.
