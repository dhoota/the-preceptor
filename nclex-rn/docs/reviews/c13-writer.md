## For the reviewer

- rn-c13-1-2 and rn-c13-1-1. ASA says voiding before discharge is needed only for selected clients. Here the surgeon's orders make it a criterion, and the client has retention. The key follows the orders tab.
- rn-c13-1-5. Diet advice after cholecystectomy (small, low-fat meals as tolerated) rests on Brunner. Practice varies and some surgeons give no restriction.
- rn-c13-2-1 and rn-c13-2-2. The 2 to 3 mm wafer margin and emptying at one third to one half full come from standard ostomy teaching in Brunner and WOCN material. Please check the exact numbers against the edition.
- rn-c13-2-4. The key excludes a psychiatric referral because the client has no history of depression and sleeps well. A reviewer may judge this borderline.
- rn-c13-3-4. The SpO2 target of 94% to 98% is from BTS. Many US units use 92% to 96%. The row is keyed on the BTS range, which is stated in `refs`.
- rn-c13-3-4. Routine corticosteroids are keyed "not anticipated" for pneumonia that is not severe, as in ATS and IDSA 2019. I set the vital signs and labs so the client does not meet severe criteria (respiratory rate 26, BUN 18 mg/dL, PaO2 54 mmHg on room air).
- rn-c13-4-5. The 2021 update prefers fidaxomicin over vancomycin for a first episode. Oral vancomycin 125 mg 4 times a day is still an accepted alternative. The item tests only the volume.
- rn-c13-4-4. Sporicidal cleaning is keyed as a plan measure. IDSA advises it mainly in outbreaks or high rates. CDC advises spore-killing products.
- rn-c13-5-1. Room 208 is keyed "suggests influenza" and then tests negative in rn-c13-5-2. That is intended.
- rn-c13-3-1. The stem was reworded to avoid a near duplicate with rn-c09-4-1.
