# Batch s31 Canada notes

Batch s31 holds 50 items in Safety and Infection Prevention and Control. The Canada pass ran on the items as they stand after review. Only the `canada` field and Canada sources were added in this pass. Stems, choices, keys and rationales were not changed for Canada.

## Counts

- Notes written: 5
- Left empty: 45

## Notes written

- rn-s31-08: The Canadian Tuberculosis Standards (2022) prefer an IGRA mainly when BCG was given after infancy or its timing is unknown. BCG given in infancy can be ignored for an adult skin test of 10 mm or more.
- rn-s31-11: The Canadian Immunization Guide uses hepatitis A vaccine for prophylaxis from 6 months of age, preferably within 14 days. Immune globulin is for infants under 6 months and is added for immunocompromised contacts.
- rn-s31-12: The Canadian Immunization Guide recommends offering prophylaxis to susceptible coworkers and clients of an infected food handler.
- rn-s31-13: The FDA Food Code is US. Food handler exclusion in Canada follows provincial rules. Nova Scotia excludes a jaundiced food handler until one week after jaundice began.
- rn-s31-42: Health Canada's Cribs, Cradles and Bassinets Regulations (SOR/2016-152) allow only stationary sides. They limit the mattress gap to 3 cm and crib mattress thickness to 15 cm.
