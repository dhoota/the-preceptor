## How the claims were checked

- Delegation: CNPS says the delegating nurse "is ultimately responsible for the decision to delegate". It cites separate provincial standards.
- Child abuse: CYFSA s. 125(1) requires a person with "reasonable grounds to suspect" to report "immediately" to a society. Section 125(3) says the person must report "directly" and "shall not rely on any other person". "Society" means a children's aid society.
- Good Samaritan: Ontario Act s. 2 covers a health care professional who gives emergency care "without reasonable expectation of compensation or reward", and not at a hospital. It excludes damages caused by gross negligence.
- Canada Health Act: insured services are hospital, physician and surgical-dental services. Hospital services include drugs "administered in the hospital" only. The Justice Laws page shows the Act was last amended on 2017-12-12.
- RPN: Nursing Act, 1991 s. 11 makes "registered practical nurse" a protected title of College members, and s. 4 sets RPN authorized acts.
- Access and directory: PHIPA s. 52(1) gives a right of access to one's own records. Section 20(4) lets a facility give a religious representative the client's name and location. The client must have given the affiliation, been offered the chance to withhold consent and not done so.
- Syphilis: HPPA s. 25 requires a "practitioner", which includes a member of the College of Nurses of Ontario, to report to the medical officer of health. This applies to a person who is not a hospital patient or outpatient. O. Reg. 135/18 lists syphilis as a disease of public health significance.
- Adult protection: Nova Scotia Adult Protection Act s. 5(1) says "Every person who has information ... indicating that an adult is in need of protection shall report that information to the Minister."
- Potassium: the MCC list (updated December 2020) gives 3.5 to 5.1 mmol/L. The item's reference range (3.5 to 5.0) is left as written. The note gives the MCC range only as Canadian context, and 2.9 mmol/L is low under both ranges.
- Long-term care: FLTCA s. 3 gives residents the right to "manage their own financial affairs" and to raise concerns "without fear of coercion, discrimination or reprisal".
- Complaints: ECFAA s. 6(1) says every health care organization "shall have a patient relations process and shall make information about that process available to the public". The item gate bans "patient" in notes, so the note says "complaints process".
- Minors: the CMPA says the age of majority is "largely irrelevant" and that capacity decides. It does not treat marriage as a factor. The note leaves out Quebec, where the age of consent is generally 14.
- Personal directives: Nova Scotia PDA s. 3(1) lets a person with capacity set out care instructions and name a delegate.
