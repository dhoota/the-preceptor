## Notes written

- rn-s32-06: Diabetes Canada preconception A1C target of 7.0% or less, ideally 6.5% or less.
- rn-s32-12 and 13: The Canadian Task Force (2014) recommends against PSA screening. The Canadian Urological Association supports shared decision making from age 50.
- rn-s32-17: Driver reporting duties are provincial. Reporting is discretionary in Alberta, Quebec and Nova Scotia.
- rn-s32-20: Canada has no federal over-the-counter hearing aid category. Access without a prescription varies by province.
- rn-s32-23: The Canadian Dental Association uses fluoride toothpaste under age 3 only with decay risk. A bedtime bottle is a listed risk factor.
- rn-s32-24: Blood lead in micromol/L, with the CDC reference value converted.
- rn-s32-25: Provincial school HPV programs, and NACI 2024 advice of 1 dose from 9 to 20 years.
- rn-s32-31 and 32: The WHO Growth Charts for Canada put obesity above the 97th percentile from 5 to 19 years.
- rn-s32-43 and 46: Every province and territory uses an 85 dBA limit. Federal workplaces use 87 dBA.
- rn-s32-45: Cervical screening varies by province. Ontario moved to HPV testing every 5 years from age 25 in 2025.
- rn-s32-48: Nine jurisdictions tell clients their breast density directly. The rest report it to the primary care provider.
- rn-s32-50: Health Canada advises pharmacy return and says not to flush medicines.
