## How the claims were checked

- Delegation: CNPS (January 2021, revised February 2024) names health care aides and home support workers as unregulated care providers. It says nurses follow the regulator's delegation standards and the tasks the employer approves.
- Practical nurse title: the CCPNR page describes its members as the regulators of "Licensed/Registered Practical Nurses". RPN is the Ontario title. The Ontario Nursing Act, 1991, s. 11(1) restricts "registered practical nurse" to members.
- Elder abuse: Nova Scotia Adult Protection Act s. 5(1) says every person with information that an adult is in need of protection "shall report that information to the Minister". Ontario's Fixing Long-Term Care Act s. 28 requires an immediate report to the Director of suspected abuse of a resident. Elder Abuse Prevention Ontario says no Ontario law requires a report when the older adult lives in their own home.
- PHIPA s. 40(1): a custodian "may disclose" if it believes on reasonable grounds that disclosure is necessary to eliminate or reduce "a significant risk of serious bodily harm to a person or group of persons". The note says "permits", matching the text.
- Safe haven: LawNow (2013) says there is no safe haven legislation anywhere in Canada. No later provincial law was found. Criminal Code s. 218 makes it an offence to unlawfully abandon a child under 10 so that its life is or is likely to be endangered. Providence Health Care describes the anonymous cradle beside the St. Paul's emergency entrance.
- Service animals: O. Reg. 191/11 s. 80.45(4) makes an animal a service animal if it is readily identified by a vest or harness, or if the person gives documentation from a listed regulated health professional.
- Workplace violence: Ontario OHSA s. 32.0.1 requires a written workplace violence policy. Section 32.0.2(2)(c) requires measures for workers to report incidents.
- Device incidents: Health Canada guidance (published June 26, 2019, in force December 16, 2019) requires hospitals to report serious medical device incidents in writing within 30 calendar days of first documentation. Its example is an infusion pump malfunction.
- Discharge: the Canada Health Act defines insured health services as hospital, physician and surgical-dental services. ECFAA s. 6(1) says every health care organization "shall have a patient relations process". The note calls this a complaint process because the gate bans the word patient in notes.
