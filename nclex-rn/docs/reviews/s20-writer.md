## Points for the reviewer

- Item 09: the left side, head-down position for air embolism follows the INS 2024 summary I found. That summary came from a secondary education page, not the full standard. Some sources now question the benefit of this position.
- Item 47: "at least 10 minutes" of pressure on anticoagulants is common textbook teaching. I could not open the exact Potter or Pagana page. Please confirm the wording.
- Item 32: the rule to repeat a newborn screen taken before 24 hours was checked on state program pages (Minnesota, Wisconsin). It is cited to Wong. Please confirm the page, or swap in a state or HRSA source.
- Item 37: the definition of post-ERCP pancreatitis (lipase at least 3 times the upper limit) is the standard consensus definition. I could not open the full ASGE text.
- Item 48: the guaiac diet limits (red meat, vitamin C above 250 mg, peroxidase-rich vegetables) come from Pagana. Newer high-sensitivity guaiac kits may need fewer limits.
- Item 35: the teaching to avoid BP cuffs on the arm where nodes were removed is still in Brunner. The evidence for it is weak.
- Item 12: the key is apple juice. I left herbal tea out because ASNC Appendix 1 lists it without a clear caffeine value.
- Item 07: the item asks about low SpO2, not fever, so it does not rely on the disputed link between atelectasis and fever.
- The Canada notes are empty. Item 04 and others already pair mEq/L with mmol/L.
