## For the reviewer

- 1-3. The item does not print a numeric phototherapy threshold. The stem says the provider judged the level above threshold for age and risk. I did not check the AAP 2022 curve value for 37 weeks at about 72 hours.
- 1-2. Birth under 38 weeks, a sibling who needed phototherapy and exclusive breastfeeding with poor intake are taken from the AAP 2022 risk factor lists. I confirmed them through secondary summaries, not the full text.
- 1-4 and 1-6. The wet diaper and feeding numbers (8 to 12 feeds, waking after 3 hours) are standard textbook teaching. I did not quote a page.
- 2-4. The key includes a same-day evaluation for passive thoughts of death with no plan or intent. ACOG No. 4 asks for immediate risk assessment and management matched to the risk. A reviewer may prefer different wording for the urgency.
- 3-2. The reference ranges in the laboratory tab are typical values for a toddler. They are not from one named laboratory. The platelet count is left out on purpose because thrombocytosis usually appears in the second week.
- 3-4. Aspirin every 6 hours matches the AHA 2017 dose schedule. The 2024 update discusses adding corticosteroids for high-risk children. I left steroids out of the options to avoid a second defensible key.
- 4-4. CPS 2017 pairs dexamethasone with nebulized epinephrine for moderate croup with stridor at rest and in-drawing. Other protocols give epinephrine only for more severe distress.
- 4-6. The 2 hour epinephrine duration and the 2 to 4 hour observation window come from CPS 2017.
- 5-2. The rows follow the ADA school statement for older elementary students. Individual children vary, which the rationale states.
- 5-3. "Alcohol and driving as a teen" is the only later-topic token. I avoided an insulin pump token because ADA 2026 supports early automated insulin delivery.
- 5-5. The insulin-to-carbohydrate ratio and the correction factor are invented for the case. They are not guideline values.
- The Canada notes are empty. Canadian units (mmol/L only) and CPS croup guidance are relevant for the later Canada pass.
