# Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** January 1, 2026
**Last updated:** August 16, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Preceptor ("we", "us", "our") handles information
in connection with your use of our applications.

**This policy applies to every application we publish** — every app released
under our Apple App Store and Google Play developer accounts, whether or not it
carries the Preceptor name. That currently includes independent study tools that
help candidates prepare for healthcare certification and licensing examinations,
and casual games, and it automatically covers any new app we release. In this
policy, "our apps" and "the app" mean any of them, and a current list is
available on our App Store and Google Play developer pages.

We are based in Ontario, Canada. We have written this policy to be clear and
honest. If anything is unclear, please contact us (see "Contact us" below).

By downloading, accessing, or using any of our apps, you acknowledge that you
have read and understood this Privacy Policy.

---

## 1. Summary (the short version)

- Our apps do not require you to create an account.
- We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, or precise location.
- Your progress is stored only on your device — we do not receive a copy.
- We do not display advertising, and we do not track you across other apps or
  websites.
- We do not sell or rent your personal information to anyone.
- The only times information leaves your device are: (a) if you use an optional
  AI feature, where an app offers one, and (b) when you make a purchase — both
  explained in detail below.

Not every app has every feature described below. Where a section applies only to
certain apps, it says so, and where an app does not have that feature, nothing in
that section happens.

---

## 2. Information we handle

### 2.1 Information you provide directly

Our apps have no sign-up or login, so we do not ask for or collect personal
details such as your name or email. If you choose to email us for support, we
will have whatever information you include in that message.

### 2.2 Your progress and settings (stored locally on your device)

To remember your progress between sessions, our apps save information on your
device only, using local on-device storage. Depending on the app, this includes:

- in study apps: which questions you have answered and whether you answered
  correctly, questions you have flagged or saved for review, and your scores and
  progress counts;
- in games: your day, level, score, in-game currency and items, and which
  upgrades, characters, story chapters and cosmetic themes you have unlocked;
- in any app: your settings, such as whether sound is muted, and whether you
  have unlocked premium content.

This data stays on your device. We do not upload, transmit, or store a copy of it
on our servers, and it is not connected to your identity. You can clear it at any
time from within the app — using a "Reset progress" option, or by starting a new
game or new session where the app offers that — or by uninstalling the app.

### 2.3 AI features (optional, and only in apps that offer them)

Some of our apps include an optional AI feature that can generate explanations or
other content. When — and only when — you request this, the relevant content you
are working on is transmitted, through a secure relay, to our third-party AI
provider to generate a response, which is then returned to you. This content:

- is used solely to produce the material you asked for;
- is not tied to your real-world identity by us;
- is not used by us to build a profile about you.

If you prefer not to transmit any content, you can simply not use the feature;
the rest of the app works without it.

Apps that do not offer an AI feature transmit no content of any kind. Several of
our apps are fully usable offline, in airplane mode, with no network connection
at any point.

### 2.4 Purchase information

Our apps are free to download. Some features or content may require an optional
purchase — for example, a one-time unlock, a subscription, or a consumable pack
of in-app currency.

Purchases are processed by the app store from which you downloaded the app (the
Apple App Store or Google Play) together with our purchase-management provider,
RevenueCat, Inc. Payment is handled entirely by those services. We never receive
or store your credit/debit card number or full payment details. We may receive a
confirmation that a purchase occurred, along with an anonymous
purchase/transaction identifier, so the app can recognize your purchase and keep
content unlocked — including if you reinstall, using the app's "Restore
Purchases" option.

This anonymous purchase record is the only information about you that we hold
anywhere other than your own device, and it is not linked to your name, email, or
any other identifier.

### 2.5 Automatically collected technical and diagnostic data

The app distribution platforms (the Apple App Store and Google Play) and the
services we use may automatically collect limited, largely aggregated technical
data — such as crash logs, app version, general device model, and app
performance/installation statistics — in accordance with their own policies. We
use this only to keep our apps stable and functioning. We do not use it to
identify you personally.

### 2.6 What we do NOT collect

We do not collect your contacts, photos, microphone or camera input, precise GPS
location, health records, or any special-category personal data. We do not
collect usage analytics or behavioural data. Our apps contain no social features,
no chat, no user profiles, and no user-generated content. Our apps do not track
you across other apps or websites.

## 3. Advertising and tracking

**Our apps show no advertising of any kind.** They contain no third-party
advertising software, no analytics or attribution software, and no cross-app
tracking technologies. We do not use the Apple Advertising Identifier (IDFA) and
we never present the App Tracking Transparency prompt, because there is nothing
to track.

## 4. How we use information

We use the limited information described above to:

- provide each app's core functionality and remember your progress on your
  device;
- generate AI content when you request it, in apps that offer it;
- process and restore your purchase and keep premium content unlocked;
- maintain, secure, debug, and improve our apps;
- comply with legal obligations and enforce our terms.

We do not use your information for advertising or for sale to third parties.

## 5. Legal bases for processing

Where Canadian privacy law (PIPEDA) applies, we handle information based on your
consent and for purposes a reasonable person would consider appropriate in the
circumstances. Where the EU/UK GDPR applies to users in those regions, our legal
bases are:

- **Performance of a contract** — to deliver the app and your purchase;
- **Consent** — for optional AI features (you choose to use them);
- **Legitimate interests** — to keep our apps secure, stable, and functioning,
  balanced against your rights.

You may withdraw consent (for example, by not using an AI feature, or by
uninstalling the app) at any time.

## 6. Service providers and third parties

We rely on a small number of reputable providers ("processors") who handle data
only as needed to provide their service:

- **Apple Inc.** — app distribution, billing, and diagnostic data for apps
  downloaded from the Apple App Store. Governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.
- **Google LLC / Google Play** — app distribution, billing, and diagnostic data
  for apps downloaded from Google Play. Governed by Google's Privacy Policy.
- **RevenueCat, Inc.** — manages and validates in-app purchases. Receives an
  anonymous purchase identifier; does not receive your card details.

Each provider operates under its own privacy policy. We encourage you to review
them. We do not authorize any of these providers to use your information for
their own independent marketing.

## 9. Data retention

Because your progress lives on your device, you control its lifetime — resetting
progress in the app or uninstalling it removes the data. We do not maintain our
own database of your activity. Purchase records and diagnostic data are retained
by the respective providers (Apple, Google, RevenueCat) according to their
policies and our legal/accounting obligations.

## 10. Security

We design our apps to keep your data on your device and to route any purchase or
AI requests through established providers that use industry-standard encryption
in transit. While no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely
secure, we take reasonable and appropriate measures to protect your information.

## 11. Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or
restrict the processing of your personal information, and to data portability or
to object to certain processing. Because we hold very little personal information
about you, you can exercise most control directly in the app:

- **Reset your progress** — clears your locally stored data, using the app's
  reset or new-game option where one is offered;
- **Don't use AI features** — prevents any content from being transmitted, in
  apps that offer them;
- **Uninstall** — removes all locally stored data from your device.

For purchase data, you can also use your Apple or Google Play account controls.
To make any other request, contact us using the details below, and we will
respond as required by applicable law. Users in the EU/UK have the right to lodge
a complaint with their local data-protection authority; users in Canada may
contact the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada.

We do not "sell" or "share" personal information as those terms are defined under
California law (CCPA/CPRA).

## 12. Children's privacy

Some of our apps — our exam-preparation study tools — are intended for healthcare
trainees and professionals and are not directed at children. Others, such as our
casual games, are rated for general audiences and may be played by children.

None of our apps are designed to collect anything from anyone. Regardless of a
user's age, our apps:

- require no account, no sign-in, and no email address;
- collect no personal information whatsoever;
- contain no advertising, no third-party analytics, and no cross-app tracking;
- contain no chat, no social features, and no user-generated content;
- store all progress locally on the device only.

The only exception is optional in-app purchases, which are processed by Apple or
Google and are subject to the parental controls, purchase approval, and
Ask-to-Buy settings those platforms provide. We encourage parents and guardians
to use them.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a
child has provided us information, please contact us and we will take
appropriate steps.
