## For the physician

- analgesia-sedation-16 q3. ESMO gives the 10 to 15% rescue rule but no oral to IV ratio for hydromorphone. The explanation's "roughly 1 to 2 mg IV" assumes an oral to IV ratio of about 2 to 3 to 1. No guideline found states that ratio for hydromorphone. The ESMO full text was behind a paywall, so the 10 to 15% figure comes from the publisher's page summary. Please confirm both.
- analgesia-sedation-16 q2 still cites Tintinalli. It was outside this task's figure. It needs a real source.
- delirium-agitation-19 q3. The task brief states that SSKI is not marketed in Canada. I did not check this against the Health Canada Drug Product Database. Please confirm that Lugol's solution is what Canadian pharmacies stock.
- cva-40 q2 cites CSBPR ICH 2020 for the 3000 unit PCC cap. The cap is confirmed by Thrombosis Canada and NAC. I could not open CSBPR ICH to confirm it says the same.
- loc-37. BTF 4th edition has no mannitol dose. The dose now comes from ENLS v6.0, a Neurocritical Care Society protocol based on consensus, not a graded guideline. No Canadian guideline gives this dose.
- loc-26. No Canadian guideline gives a bicarbonate dose for tricyclic toxicity. The Emergency Care BC summary is a clinical summary, not a formal guideline. The dose and pH target are consensus figures, backed by a 2016 literature review and the AHA 2023 update.
- ems-27. Ontario ALS PCS v5.5 is in consultation. The proposed changes do not list the nerve agent directive. Recheck the doses when v5.5 comes into force. Hospital pediatric IV pralidoxime dosing (bolus and infusion) is not tested in this item and was not sourced.
- ems-29 q3 was listed as a tricyclic bicarbonate item. It is a neonatal HIE item. If the neonatal bicarbonate teaching point is meant to stay, it needs a neonatal resuscitation source.
- Octaplex and Beriplex product monographs. Beriplex (Canada) doses by INR at 25, 35 and 50 units/kg, with caps of 2500, 3500 and 5000 units. Octaplex doses in mL/kg by INR band, with a 3000 IU single dose cap. NAC notes these regimens are rarely used in practice. No SAMP keys a monograph dose.
