- **Settings that look alike.** ischemic-heart-disease-24 and ischemic-heart-disease-19 both place an older woman with atypical symptoms 3 hours from PCI. The first is a 2-hour STEMI given half-dose tenecteplase, the second a completed infarct 30 hours old. ischemic-heart-disease-23 and loc-30 both involve a woman who collapses with a thunderclap headache from SAH. The first is keyed on ECG changes and cardiac workup, the second on grading and drainage.
- **Paywalled guidelines.** ESC, AHA/ACC and some OUP guideline texts could not be opened in full. Where that happened, an open source was used instead, such as the Widimsky registry for new RBBB, the Brady HEART review and the SSC 2021 full text. The IDSA SSTI 2014 and IDSA Lyme 2020 recommendations were read on the publisher page.
- **Claims outside the cited source.** A few distractor explanations use standard teaching that the cited source does not state. Examples are intraosseous access as a rapid route in adults (infectious-diseases-27 q1), iron and erythropoietin not correcting an acute fall (infectious-diseases-27 q4), and the ages for Perthes disease and slipped epiphysis (infectious-diseases-37 q1).
- **Non-Canadian sources.** Canadian guidance was used where it exists. Some questions rely on US or international sources because no Canadian one covers the point: ACOG for listeriosis in pregnancy, the IDSA 2026 AMR guidance for Enterobacter cloacae, the IDSA Lyme guideline and STREAM-2 for half-dose tenecteplase at 75 years and older.
- **Judgement calls worth a look.** ischemic-heart-disease-42 discharges a young woman with type 1 diabetes at HEART 2 with family physician follow-up, as the HEART review supports. Some clinicians would add outpatient ischemia testing. infectious-diseases-37 keys no chemoprophylaxis for household contacts, including a pregnant mother, because the CPS practice point limits it to severe invasive GAS.
- **Mock mode.** Every update, prompt and option was checked so that it does not name or imply an earlier key. In some SAMPs this meant vaguer updates, such as "her initial tests are unremarkable" in ischemic-heart-disease-42.
