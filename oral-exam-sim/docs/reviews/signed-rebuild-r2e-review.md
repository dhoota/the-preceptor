## The coordinator's specific questions

- **seizures-05 q3, one case letter.** Not enough by itself. The practice is mainstream toxicology, so the key can stay. But the only source is a single case whose authors say beta blockers "may be inadvisable" in exactly this unstable situation. The writer should open and cite a standard reference (see the q3 row).
- **seizures-07 q4, avoiding epinephrine.** The claim is quoted correctly. Fitzgerald 2025 (PMC12772588, full text) says: "early management with epinephrine may be counterproductive or even dangerous by sustaining catecholamine drive and causing or sustaining a VT storm. Early use of opioids, sedation and general anaesthesia may be very effective." That is a discussion statement in a 32-child cohort, citing Bellamy 2019 (Pediatr Crit Care Med, "The Cardiac Arrest Where Epinephrine Is Contraindicated"). It is adequate support for teaching it, but thin for a key that overrides ACLS and PALS in arrest. The writer should also cite Bellamy 2019 directly. The key's "even if he arrests" goes beyond the scenario, where he has a pulse, and Arjan should confirm it. The question fails anyway, on the repeat and the match defects.
- **shock-04 q3, saline over Ringer's because of a small subdural.** Not fair. The European recommendation is for severe head trauma, and the same recommendation accepts balanced crystalloid. On top of that, EASL's own algorithm supports 20% albumin for his stage 2 AKI.
- **shock-05, autotransfusion in Canadian rural practice.** It is defensible. Chapman supports it in low-resource settings with significant delay. European Recommendation 17 suggests cell salvage for thoracic bleeding. Many trauma chest drain systems allow reinfusion. Arjan should confirm that rural Canadian EDs stock such drains, because the stem has to tell the candidate they are available. It must say so without naming the answer.
- **shock-05, norepinephrine as a bridge.** Acceptable as a last-resort bridge. It is European Grade 1C, and the rationale specifically endorses transient noradrenaline below a systolic of 80. The explanation already gives the harm caveat. The unacceptable "more crystalloid" should go.
- **shock-11 q4, select 1.** The kind is `menu` before and after. `select` is a parameter of the menu kind, and SAMP_SPEC allows `select` 1 to 4. Round 1 recommended exactly this change. The kind is kept.
