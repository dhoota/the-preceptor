## For the reviewers

- anaphylaxis-48 q4: WAO writes the tryptase rule as 1.2 x baseline + 2. Units are given as mcg/L, which equals ng/mL.
- anaphylaxis-49 q3: the dose is 0.01 mg/kg from the 1 mg/mL ampoule. A 0.15 mg autoinjector was deliberately left out of the options because CSACI accepts it for children under 15 kg when nothing else is available.
- anaphylaxis-51 q2: the infusion rate is keyed to the Resuscitation Council UK algorithm, named in the update, because North American references give other starting regimens.
- anaphylaxis-52 q1: xanthan gum allergy exists but is rare. The key rests on buckwheat being a known severe allergen that made up the batter.
- anaphylaxis-50 q2 cites a tilt-table study in people aged 15 and over. Applying its jerk count to a fall at home is an extrapolation.
