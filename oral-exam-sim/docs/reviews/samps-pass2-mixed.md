## For the physician

- lacerations-34 q3: the NEON trial (2025, adults, stopped early and underpowered) found no clear benefit of suture repair over alignment for digital nerves. The key is still hand surgery referral, but the explanation no longer says the nerve must be repaired. Please confirm this for a 2-year-old.
- lacerations-31 q1: the TQIP source supports the open fracture half of the key. The loss of toe extension means cut extensor tendons half rests on the examination.
- lacerations-23 q1: the distractor reasons (Vibrio from salt water, Aeromonas from fresh water, Eikenella from human bites, Pseudomonas from wet footwear) are standard teaching and are not in the CDC page.
- lacerations-16 q1: the crossed fingers test is not named in Dy 2016. The review supports intrinsic weakness and the ulnar sensory territory.
- eye-23: traumatic optic neuropathy is new to the bank. The key (observation, no routine high dose steroids) follows the Cochrane reviews. Practice varies, so please confirm.
- eye-34 q1: the guideline is a 2015 NHS paediatric document whose review date has passed. The claim that acetone and ethanol injure the cornea is not in it.
- eye-37 q2: that ondansetron has no anticholinergic effect is standard pharmacology. Lachkar 2007 supports the risk from drugs with anticholinergic effects as a class.
- pre-eclampsia-23 q1: the SOGC 431 full text could not be retrieved (403), so FIGO 2022 is cited. The ergonovine dose of 0.2 mg follows FIGO. The Canadian product is labelled 0.25 mg/mL, so please confirm the dose in the option. The carboprost note (no asthma) is standard teaching and is not quoted from FIGO.
- pre-eclampsia-23 q2: the 12 hour lab interval is carried over from the earlier ACOG PB 222 wording, now applied after birth. Please confirm.
- pre-eclampsia-34 q2: the key is 60 minutes, the upper end of ACOG's 30 to 60 minute window.
- pre-eclampsia-18 q1: Ramoska 1989 is old but is primary ED literature and is still cited.
