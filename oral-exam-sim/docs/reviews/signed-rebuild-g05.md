# Signed-duplicate rebuilds, group g05

Files: `src/samps/s16/shock.ts` and `src/samps/s16/seizures.ts`. Ten SAMPs rebuilt: seizures-03, seizures-05, seizures-07, shock-04, shock-05, shock-07, shock-09, shock-11, shock-12, shock-13.

Every rebuilt SAMP keeps its id, topic, question count, and each question's kind and key feature. Each one now has `reviewed: false` after the META spread and `version: 2`. All ten are held back in `src/samps/held-back/g05.json`. No other SAMP was edited. Old source entries in each file's `S` table were left in place.

Checks: `SAMP_BATCH=s16 npx vitest run tests/samps.test.ts tests/samp-quality.test.ts` passes (465 tests). `npx tsc --noEmit -p .` is clean. The s16 single-answer key positions are now 2, 4, 2, 5, 5 (18 singles, highest 28%), and the key is the longest option in 5 of 18.

## How duplicates were checked

- Decision search: each keyed decision's core terms were searched in `scratchpad/allkeys.tsv` and in `src/samps` (live files, including other writers' current work).
- Similarity: title, stem, updates and keys of each draft were run through `simcheck.py` with the SAMP's own id excluded. The digest still holds the old text of my own ids, so an old copy of one of my ids can appear as a neighbour.
- Four suggested directions were dropped because the bank already has the story. Venous air embolism is shock-28. Massive hemothorax after rib fractures on apixaban is shock-29. The nephrotic child with peritonitis is infectious-diseases-26, and stress-dose hydrocortisone for a child is shock-43. Theophylline with charcoal and levels is tox-28. The paced older adult bleeding on apixaban was too close to shock-29.
- Two replacement ideas for shock-11 were also dropped during the final re-grep. Another writer's current ems-07 is cyanide from bitter apricot kernels, and tox-16 is a hydroxychloroquine overdose in a woman with lupus.

## For the physician

- All ten need a fresh sign-off. They are held back in `src/samps/held-back/g05.json`.
- Some keyed decisions repeat keys that are already in the bank, even though the stories are new:
  - shock-05 q2 (MTP, ratio, restricted crystalloid, permissive target) and q3 (calcium chloride 1 g) match keys in multiple-trauma-11, -01 and -32.
  - shock-09 q2 (balanced crystalloid 30 mL/kg) matches shock-03.
  - seizures-03 q3 (lipid emulsion, small epinephrine doses, bypass) matches the lidocaine toxicity SAMPs of signed cluster 27 (analgesia-sedation-04, lacerations-08). The approved direction asked for this key. The new decisions are in q2 (hydralazine because beta blockers and calcium channel blockers are avoided in LAST) and q4.
  - shock-12 q3 (clindamycin with anti-staphylococcal cover) matches infectious-diseases-07 and pediatric-fever-15.
  - shock-13 q1 shares the return-to-flow MAP and controller readings with ems-18. The direction named ems-18 and approved this overlap. I removed a "no CPR" answer so that ems-18's key is not repeated.
- shock-09 is septic shock, not the obstructive shock in the direction, because the suggested story (air embolism) is shock-28. Its vasopressor advice for a patient taking an MAO inhibitor draws on the Nardil monograph's overdose section: fluids, then a dilute pressor titrated carefully, because adrenergic agents may give a markedly increased pressor response. Please confirm that this is the teaching you want.
- In seizures-03, the statement that labetalol, nifedipine and hydralazine are the usual first-line drugs for severe hypertension in pregnancy is not cited. I could not open the SOGC 2022 guideline this session (the publisher returned 403), so I cited only the ASRA checklist and the review of local anesthetic toxicity. Left uterine displacement is accepted as an answer in q3, but no opened source covers it.
- The aconite evidence is case reports, case series and a review of case reports. IV flecainide may not be available in Canada, so the key accepts it "where it can be given", and amiodarone is the practical answer.
- shock-13 relies on a 2020 LVAD review and a 2020 ED review. The 2023 ISHLT guideline was not open access.
- For warfarin reversal, shock-13 uses the NAC sample dosing (2000 IU for an INR of 3 to 5, or 25 IU/kg for an INR of 2 to under 4). The key does not set a vitamin K dose.
- The caffeine source is a Japanese registry follow-up. Its proposed hemodialysis triggers include a caffeine level of 140 mg/L or more, which few labs can report quickly.
- shock-09 no longer lists dvt-pe in `alsoTopics`, because the new case has no PE content.
