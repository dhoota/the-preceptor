## For the physician

- **All seven ids keep their signed text.** Under the round 2 rules none of them has a fresh keyed decision for every preserved key feature. The blocks are delirium KF 4 (sedation before restraint) for 04, 05 and 11, seizures KF 4 for 14, seizures KF 2 for 15, and tox KF 2 with KF 7 for 06. In 09 the only story that fit had already been signed as tox-17.
- **Delirium KF 4 is saturated.** Every sedating agent sold in Canada for agitation is now keyed in a signed SAMP. A new question under delirium KF 4 can only repeat one. If you want these ids rebuilt, one option is to allow one KF 4 repeat per SAMP explicitly, outside D1. Another is to let a rebuild change a question's key feature.
- **Two fresh decisions found here are worth keeping for later work.** (1) Medetomidine withdrawal, now reported across the fentanyl supply in Pennsylvania (MMWR 2025). It resists opioids, benzodiazepines and phenobarbital, responds to a dexmedetomidine infusion, and is underscored by the COWS, which does not score blood pressure or mental status. It is keyed nowhere, but its sedation step needs dexmedetomidine, which the tox-15 key already accepts as an adjunct. (2) The BC temporary substitute rule (s. 16(2)(b)). A child who has had no contact in the preceding 12 months does not qualify, and a close friend ranks above a relative by marriage. No SAMP keys a substitute rule outside Ontario.
- **Keyword search misses stories whose keys omit the drug name.** tox-17 keys "Venous blood gas for PCO2" and "Hold further patches", so searches of the keys for "fentanyl" and "patch" did not find it. Simcheck did. Future directions should run simcheck on the story idea before any drafting.
