## Sources and verification

I verified each source with WebSearch or WebFetch, or by looking up its PMID or DOI on the Europe PMC record. Every URL was opened or resolved to the right record.

- Zhou Y and colleagues, Clin Ophthalmol 2022. I read the full text at dovepress.com. It supports the shield, no tonometry, antiemetic, morphine, NPO, CT and no MRI, vancomycin 15 mg/kg with ceftazidime 50 mg/kg, tetanus and repair within 24 hours.
- Canadian Immunization Guide, tetanus toxoid chapter, updated June 2026. Booster at 5 or more years for wounds other than clean minor ones, Tdap from age 7, no immune globulin with 3 or more doses.

- SOGC Guideline No. 414, 2021, PMID 33453378, and No. 448, 2024, PMID 38553007. Checked on Europe PMC. The Rh decision follows ruling A18.
- McGee S and colleagues, JAMA 1999. Abstract confirms the postural pulse rise of 30 or more.
- WHO 2013 intimate partner violence guidelines (who.int page). Glass N and colleagues, J Emerg Med 2008, PMID 17961956. Abstract confirms the sixfold and sevenfold odds. Campbell JC and colleagues, Danger Assessment, 2009, PMID 18667689.

It supports halving tenecteplase over 75, the 120 minute rule, the 10 minute goal, transfer straight after lysis, angiography at 2 to 24 hours, rescue PCI at under 50% ST resolution, clopidogrel 300 mg or 75 mg over 75, and a lower enoxaparin dose at 75 or older.

## Uncertain points for the reviewer

1. Cryoprecipitate 10 units with a fibrinogen target of 1.5 g/L or more after thrombolysis. This comes from tables derived from Yaghi 2017 and the AHA/ASA stroke tables, which I saw only in secondary summaries. The Yaghi abstract confirms early correction of the coagulopathy but not the exact dose. The case extrapolates stroke thrombolysis guidance to tenecteplase given for STEMI.
2. Enoxaparin 0.75 mg/kg SC with no bolus at 75 or older. The ESC 2023 text confirms only "a lower dose" with fibrinolysis in older patients. The exact figure is from the ExTRACT-TIMI 25 regimen and was not read in a source this session.
3. ASA 160 mg chewed. This is within the ESC loading range of 150 to 300 mg. The ESC fibrinolysis section says 162 to 325 mg.
4. Protamine about 1 mg per mg of enoxaparin given within 8 hours. This is standard practice, but the ICH guideline only says protamine "may be considered" for partial reversal and gives no dose ratio.
5. I did not verify the full text of the CCS 2019 update. It is cited only for the 120 minute pharmacoinvasive pathway (item i-m1).
6. Ondansetron 4 mg for a 32 kg child is 0.125 mg/kg. That is inside Zhou's 0.15 mg/kg limit and the usual paediatric single dose cap.
7. Rh immune globulin after abdominal trauma at 6 weeks. I applied A18 (not routinely required before 8 weeks). I did not check whether SOGC 448 makes a separate trauma exception this early.
8. In the cannabis case, the item on the poison centre call and the hold on hydrochlorothiazide while sodium is 131 mmol/L both rest on general practice. The Health Canada document supports supportive care only.
9. The web search budget ran out partway through. Later checks used direct page fetches and Europe PMC lookups by PMID or DOI, not keyword searches.
