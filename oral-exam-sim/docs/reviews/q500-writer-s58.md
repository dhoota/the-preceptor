## For the reviewers

- 48 q3 uses the UK JBDS guideline because Diabetes Canada says only that fluids in HHS should be individualized. The Diabetes Canada DKA recommendation is also 0.9% saline.
- 49 q4 cites the TREKK sepsis summary for the principle that hypotension is a late sign in children. The infant is not labelled septic.
- 50 q2 rests on the US ATS/IDSA guideline. No current Canadian adult CAP guideline was found.
- 51 q4: controlled pericardial aspiration in dissection is supported only by observational data and a class IIa ESC statement, reported through the ESC review. The intra-aortic balloon pump contraindication is standard teaching and is not in the cited source.
- 52 q3: andexanet alfa now has Health Canada approval, which postdates the 2022 NAC document. The prompt therefore asks for the choice when a specific agent is unavailable.
- 52 q4: CRASH-2 enrolled adults with significant hemorrhage after trauma. Its applicability to a low-energy fragility fracture in an 81-year-old is a judgement call.
