## Residual uncertainty

- The lorazepam 0.1 mg/kg (maximum 4 mg) dose appears in the AES algorithm figure, which could not be read as text. It matches the RAMPART adult dose quoted in the guideline text and the CPS table.
- seizures-52 q1 tests "not yet status" in a patient with two seizures and full recovery. Older definitions agree. A physician should confirm the wording "Recurrent seizures without status".
- seizures-50 q1 and q2 both test recognition of the same seizure type. They are kept because they use different lead-ins (diagnosis and discriminating feature).
