## Uncertain points for reviewers

1. 39 q4: the magnesium regimen rests on the Pritchard regimen as described by Brookfield and the WHO. SOGC 426 could not be opened to confirm that it endorses IM dosing. Please confirm that the IM regimen is acceptable Canadian practice for a nursing station with no pump.
2. 39 q2 keys HELLP as the diagnosis to exclude first, over acute fatty liver of pregnancy. AFLP is not among the options. Glucose is normal.
3. 40 q4 says high-dose dexamethasone does not improve maternal outcomes in HELLP. NICE 1.8.10 is the basis. SOGC 426 was not checked on this point.
4. 41 q1: the NICE definition of pre-eclampsia says "after 20 weeks". I applied it postpartum, as ACOG does. Methyldopa's link with low mood is stated as a known side effect. NICE gives the 2 day stop rule but not the reason.
5. 41 q3: magnesium is keyed for postpartum pre-eclampsia with neurological features and a BP below the severe range. ACOG supports this. Please check that it matches SOGC 426.
6. 42 q3 says epinephrine raises BP through its alpha effect. This is basic pharmacology. WAO 2020 does not state it.
7. 43 q4: the enoxaparin dose is about 1 mg/kg at 98 kg, rounded to 100 mg. The SOAP 24 hour neuraxial interval was not reopened this session.
8. 42 q4 calls the 2 hour change "progressive pre-eclampsia". It rests on NICE's wording on progressive deterioration. It is not a named diagnostic category.
