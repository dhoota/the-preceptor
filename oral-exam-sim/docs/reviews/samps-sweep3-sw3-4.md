- The second draft used hypernatremia at 168 mmol/L in an older woman with dementia and poor intake. shock-14, signed off, is the same story and keys a saline bolus, the free water deficit and a fall of about 10 mmol/L per 24 hours. Rewritten again, to the fluoroquinolone reaction now in the file.

Residual overlaps the physician should weigh.

- tox-40 and airway-38 share the agent, sodium hydroxide swallowed. The patients differ, 58-year-old man with an accidental workplace ingestion against a 26-year-old woman with a deliberate one, and no decision is shared. airway-38 keys hoarseness with stridor and awake flexible intubation. tox-40 keys decontamination that is not done, imaging, the early complication and the operation. Confidence that they read as separate cases is medium to high.
- tox-42 sits near tox-06 and tox-23 because all three use an osmolal gap. tox-06 is ethylene glycol from a garage with fomepizole, tox-23 is ethanol in a toddler from hand sanitizer. Neither keys an iatrogenic solvent, and neither keys stopping an infusion. Medium to high.
- delirium-agitation-24 sits near delirium-agitation-35 and delirium-agitation-16, which are also drug induced delirium. Those key a bladder scan with haloperidol, and pregabalin accumulation in kidney disease. Mine keys a medication change list, a drug class effect at a normal dose with normal kidney function, what to record about capacity, and a class switch. Medium.

## For the physician

- tox-40 keys contrast enhanced CT as the test that guides care and an operation when the wall does not enhance. The World Society of Emergency Surgery and the French series support a CT led algorithm, while the Indian Society of Gastroenterology keeps endoscopy first. Endoscopy is not offered as an option in that question, so the key holds under either approach, but you may wish to confirm the local pathway before sign-off.
- delirium-agitation-32 keys lorazepam 0.5 mg IV for an agitated 68-year-old whose QTc is 520 ms with a normal potassium and magnesium. The reasoning is that every listed antipsychotic lengthens the QT further. A 2025 systematic review in the Journal of the American Geriatrics Society reports that benzodiazepines, midazolam above all, carry more adverse events than haloperidol in older adults with severe agitation. The trade off is deliberate and deserves a physician's view.
- delirium-agitation-23 turns on Alberta law. Section 9 of the Personal Directives Act sets the declaration of incapacity, section 10.1 the determination that capacity has returned, and section 24 emergency care where no agent can be reached. The alternate agent's authority is the practice reading of a directive that designates more than one agent rather than a section quoted word for word.
- tox-42 assumes the standard lactate assay reports the L form only, so the unmeasured D lactate from propylene glycol widens the anion gap beyond the reported lactate. Confirm this holds for the laboratory the app's users work in.
- delirium-agitation-24 keys stopping ciprofloxacin and choosing another class with the culture result. It does not name the replacement agent, since prostatitis coverage depends on local resistance.
