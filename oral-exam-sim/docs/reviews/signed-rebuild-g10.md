Each now has `reviewed: false`, version 2 and a neutral title. All ten are listed in `src/samps/held-back/g10.json`, so they are out of the release and their old sign-off is revoked.

Files: `src/samps/s03/anaphylaxis.ts`, `src/samps/s03/arrhythmia.ts`, `src/samps/s07/loc.ts`. No other SAMP was changed. The keepers were not touched.

## Changes from the rebuild directions

The directions were starting ideas. A bank search found five of them already taken, so those five use a different story.

- anaphylaxis-06: alteplase angioedema on ramipril is already cva-31 (stop the alteplase, antihistamines, stridor). Rebuilt as laryngeal-only anaphylaxis to soy in a woman with birch pollen allergy.
- anaphylaxis-15: a man who wants the crew to leave repeats ems-05 (a 58-year-old man, a base hospital patch, refusal and capacity keys). The refusal was dropped. The patch now leads to Kounis syndrome during transport.
- arrhythmia-04: loperamide cardiotoxicity is already tox-31 (torsades, bicarbonate 2 mmol/kg, recurrent dysrhythmia). Rebuilt as citalopram overdose.
- arrhythmia-15: donepezil bradycardia is already arrhythmia-36. Rebuilt as grayanotoxin (mad honey) poisoning.
- loc-09: an artery of Percheron infarct is already cva-25. Rebuilt as deep cerebral venous thrombosis.
- loc-08: this keeps the dispensing error from the direction. The naloxone infusion key was replaced, because loc-02, tox-17 and suicide-risk-10 already key an infusion.

## For the physician

- All ten need a fresh sign-off. The old attestations no longer apply.
- Three questions repeat a signed-off decision under D1, because each repeated decision is the literal wording of a key feature. arrhythmia-06 q1 repeats bicarbonate for a TCA (tox-03). arrhythmia-04 q2 repeats magnesium for torsades (arrhythmia-05). loc-08 q3 repeats naloxone for opioid toxicity (loc-02).
- loc-05 cites the 2021 ERC-ESICM post-resuscitation guideline. The 2025 update exists (Nolan, Intensive Care Med 2025), but it could not be opened. Please confirm that the EEG, levetiracetam or valproate, and 72-hour prognostication points are unchanged.
- loc-10 rests on a retrospective Australian series and one case report, because no Canadian guidance on guanfacine exists. The fluid bolus of 10 to 20 mL/kg and atropine 0.02 mg/kg are standard paediatric doses that these sources do not state.
- anaphylaxis-02: the protamine dose follows the Thrombosis Canada method (1 200 + 600 + 300 units = 2 100 units, about 21 mg). The stem gives 20 mg. Please confirm protamine as the reversal for a UFH-associated ICH in this ED scenario.
- anaphylaxis-06 q2 maps "features beyond the mouth" to anaphylaxis KF 2. anaphylaxis-15 q2 keys ECG transmission and a PCI destination, not ASA or nitroglycerin. Nitroglycerin is marked dangerous at a systolic BP of 86 mmHg.
- arrhythmia-06 q3 tests how cold changes drug use (ERC). It was chosen to avoid repeating environmental-05's rewarming keys.
- loc-09 q5 keys airway indications in CVT. Please check that intubation at a GCS of 7 with snoring and vomiting is the answer you want.
- The old loperamide direction for arrhythmia-04 and the donepezil direction for arrhythmia-15 were not used, because tox-31 and arrhythmia-36 already hold them. The same applies to the alteplase angioedema direction for anaphylaxis-06 (cva-31) and the Percheron direction for loc-09 (cva-25).
