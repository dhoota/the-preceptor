## For the reviewers

- first-trimester-bleeding-41 q3: SOGC says anti-D may be omitted when paternity is certain and the father is documented Rh D negative. This is a "may", so the key is the shared decision, and the explanation states she may still choose it.
- first-trimester-bleeding-43 q3 sits close to first-trimester-bleeding-09 (anti-D optional at 11 weeks). The new point is that ultrasound dating moves her from the 300 mcg range to the optional range.
- first-trimester-bleeding-43 q2: only the abstract of SOGC Guideline No. 388 was available. The statement that first trimester crown-rump length is more accurate than menstrual dates is standard, but please confirm the wording.
- first-trimester-bleeding-41 q4 depression screening is sourced to the SOGC news release, because the full Guideline No. 460 was not open access.
- The MSF guide is international field guidance. It is used for examination findings and definitions, not for Canadian treatment thresholds.
- SOGC 448 does not recommend blood typing before 8 weeks, which some older bank explanations may contradict. None of the new items depend on this.
