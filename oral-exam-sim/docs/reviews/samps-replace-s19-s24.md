## For the physician

- analgesia-sedation-33 keys flumazenil in a 4-year-old. The Canadian product monograph says safety in children under 18 is not established. The pediatric dose comes from the Atlantic Canada Poison Centre and the AAP and AAPD sedation guideline. Please confirm this is acceptable teaching.
- abdominal-pain-20 keys normal saline over Ringer's lactate because of hypochloremic alkalosis from vomiting. Ringer's lactate is not offered as an option, to avoid two defensible answers.
- abdominal-pain-36 relies on SOGC 426 for delivery in HELLP and magnesium, which I could not open. The keys are standard, but please confirm.
- airway-25 q2 keys an induction that keeps the child breathing. This reflects the CAFG approach to upper airway obstruction and usual anesthesia practice in bacterial tracheitis, not a tracheitis-specific trial.
- airway-27 q3 keys lowering the ventilator rate for a PaCO2 of 22 mmHg in pregnancy. Lapinsky gives the normal pregnant PaCO2 of 28 to 32 mmHg and warns about alkalosis.
- airway-47 draws the intubation advice from the HCM guideline's recommendation for phenylephrine and against inotropes in obstructive disease. The guideline does not address induction agents, so no induction drug is keyed.
- anaphylaxis-17 follows the Canadian Blood Services chapter that the link between IgA deficiency and anaphylaxis is weak, so washed red cells, not IgA-deficient products, are keyed.
- anaphylaxis-25 q1 keys a manually drawn 0.5 mg dose with a longer needle after an autoinjector failed in a woman with a BMI of 46. RCUK says some adults need a 38 mm needle.
- arrhythmia-31 keys temperature control between 32 and 37.5°C from the 2025 AHA post-arrest guideline, based on a summary of that guideline rather than the full text.
- arrhythmia-32 keys stopping clarithromycin and reviewing, not stopping, methadone. Please confirm this matches your practice for methadone patients with a QTc of 540 ms.
- airway-22 q2 still cites the NRP 8th edition for tube size. Please update it if you have the 9th edition lesson.
