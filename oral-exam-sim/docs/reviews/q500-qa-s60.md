## Writer flags adjudicated

- Pediatric furosemide without a dose (39 q3, 40 q1). Acceptable. No option in either question gives a dose, so specificity matches. Ong 2022 supports loop diuretics in renal impairment and Price 2019 supports loop diuretics first line.
- Ottawa Heart Failure Risk Scale (41 q2). Items and points confirmed: stroke or TIA 1, intubation for respiratory distress 2, HR 110 or more on arrival 2, SaO2 under 90% on arrival 1, HR 110 or more on the 3-minute walk (or too ill to walk) 1, new ischemic ECG changes 2, urea 12 mmol/L or more 1, serum CO2 35 mmol/L or more 2, troponin at MI level 2, NT-proBNP 5 000 or more 1 (maximum 15). Her total: TIA 1 + arrival HR 116 = 2 + SaO2 88% = 1 + urea 14.2 = 1, all else 0, total 5. Key correct. The 2017 validation abstract confirms an admission threshold of more than 1 with sensitivity 91.8% (95.8% with NT-proBNP).
- 42 q3 target 88 to 92%. Kept. BTS target for patients at risk of hypercapnic failure. She is hypercapnic with acidosis.
