# Signed-off SAMPs: Tintinalli citations made checkable

Scope: the 14 Tintinalli source objects in batches s01 to s17. The physician ruled that these SAMPs stay signed off. Only the citation string of each Tintinalli object changed. No key, stem, option, explanation, figure, source id, question source field, other source object or reviewed flag changed. No object was split.

Each new citation names the work with all seven editors, the 9th edition, McGraw Hill and 2020, and then the chapter number and title of each chapter that the citing questions rely on.

## How the chapters were verified

AccessMedicine and AccessEmergency Medicine return 403 to this environment, and the Wayback Machine is blocked here. The chapter numbers and titles therefore come from two publisher pages on the book's official McGraw Hill site. Every chapter was checked against both, and all 303 chapter entries agree between them.

1. Table of contents PDF, front matter pages vii to xiv of the 9th edition (the file is stamped 8/5/19 and gives the 9th edition video password): https://www.tintinalliem.com/chapters/Tintinalli_TOC.pdf
2. Chapter References 9th Edition page, which lists every chapter by number and title: https://www.tintinalliem.com/references_9e.php

Where a web search also returned the 9e chapter page on AccessMedicine, the row gives that link as a second pointer. Those pages could be seen only as search results, not opened.

Airway management in the 9th edition is split into Chapter 29A, Tracheal Intubation and Chapter 29B, Mechanical Ventilation. Both parts are named that way in the TOC and on the references page.

The chapter chosen for each question is my match of the question's topic to the chapter titles. I could not read the chapter text, so I could not confirm that a given figure or claim is in that chapter. No figure or key was changed.

- s09/environmental.ts: the old text named diving disorders. No question citing this object is about diving, so no diving chapter is listed. The antipsychotic chapter was added because environmental-03 (neuroleptic malignant syndrome) cites this object.
- s10/eye.ts: the old text named ocular trauma. The 9th edition has no separate ocular trauma chapter. Eye trauma is covered in Chapter 241, Eye Emergencies. Chapter 239 was added for the apixaban reversal in eye-11 q3, and Chapter 122 for the two children (eye-10 and eye-14).

## For the physician

Chapters I could not verify: none. Every chapter number and title in the new citations is on both publisher pages above.

I could not read the chapter text, so for every row it is still unconfirmed that the chapter holds the figure or claim. These are the places where the cited claim may not be in Tintinalli 9th edition at all, or may be in a different chapter:

1. ems-06 q2: benzodiazepine analogues and xylazine in the Canadian unregulated opioid supply. This spread mostly after the 2019 manuscript date, so Chapter 186, Opioids is unlikely to hold it. It is also a Canadian claim, not a Tintinalli one.
2. eye-11 q3: "Prothrombin complex concentrate is widely used in Canada" is a Canadian practice claim. Chapter 239 may cover apixaban reversal in general but not that statement.
3. ems-14 q2 and q3: MDMA toxicity is probably in Chapter 187, Cocaine and Amphetamines or Chapter 188, Hallucinogens, which the old citation did not name and I did not add. The 3% saline dose (100 mL bolus, up to three times) may not be in Chapter 17, Fluids and Electrolytes as written in 2020.
4. ems-12 q3: the JumpSTART rescue breath step. It is probably in Chapter 5, Disaster Preparedness, but I could not confirm that the chapter describes JumpSTART.
5. abdominal-pain-10 q4: the 20 mL/kg bolus may sit in Chapter 132, Fluid and Electrolyte Therapy in Infants and Children rather than Chapter 133.
6. pre-eclampsia-09 q1 and pre-eclampsia-12 q3: peripartum cardiomyopathy may be in Chapter 55, Cardiomyopathies and Pericardial Disease. Acute fatty liver of pregnancy may be in Chapter 80, Hepatic Disorders. Chapter 100 is the only chapter cited.
7. sob-03: pediatric airway foreign body. It may be in Chapter 127, Wheezing in Infants and Children as well as or instead of Chapter 126.
8. lacerations-09: high pressure injection injury. It may be in Chapter 283, Nontraumatic Disorders of the Hand rather than Chapter 268.
9. environmental-14 q2: fetal monitoring after a minor household shock in pregnancy. Chapter 219 may not address it. Chapter 256, Trauma in Pregnancy is another possible home.
10. analgesia-sedation-10 q3: nerve blocks for a patient on buprenorphine, and the advice to discuss blocks with orthopedics because of compartment syndrome. Chapter 35 may not hold the buprenorphine part in its 2020 form.
