## Points for the reviewers

1. Units on drug concentrations. tox-19 uses a valproate of 6800 umol/L and tox-20 a carbamazepine of 165 umol/L rising to 220 umol/L. The EXTRIP papers state their thresholds in mg/L, so please confirm the SI conversions read correctly for a Canadian laboratory.
2. tox-46 restarts methadone at 45 mg after 4 missed daily doses, taken from the guidance that gives about half the previous dose or a usual starting dose, whichever is higher. Provincial programs vary, so please confirm the number and the 3 hour observation.
3. tox-24 keys supportive care rather than naloxone for a clonidine exposure. The evidence for naloxone here is inconsistent, and the explanation says so rather than calling it harmful.
4. tox-27 q1 is a menu keying hydroxocobalamin and high concentration oxygen. Sodium thiosulfate was deliberately left out of the option list because some Canadian centres give it alongside hydroxocobalamin and it would have been a second defensible answer.
5. tox-33 q3 keys "None" for extracorporeal removal of colchicine. Please confirm that reads as intended in the app.
6. tox-39 keys intubation for a deeply unresponsive patient with a snoring airway and a respiratory rate of 10/minute. Some centres would position and observe a suspected gamma hydroxybutyrate exposure, so the stem was written with inadequate ventilation to make the airway decision clear.
7. tox-35 keys reduced dose epinephrine of about 50 mcg for a 52 kg patient in arrest from local anesthetic toxicity. Please check the dose against the current checklist wording.
8. The empty test suite named "signed-off answer keys" reports no tests for any expansion batch, including s19, when SAMP_BATCH is set. It is unrelated to this batch and did not fail in the combined run above.

Points for the reviewers on the new cases:

1. tox-21 keys calcium first at a potassium of 7.6 mmol/L with a PR of 240 ms and a QRS of 118 ms, then whole bowel irrigation for the sustained release tablets and transfer for dialysis when the potassium climbs to 7.9 mmol/L with new weakness. Sixty tablets of 20 mmol is 1200 mmol of potassium. Please confirm the calcium gluconate dose of 3 g reads correctly for your practice.
2. tox-28 deliberately avoids keying an EXTRIP theophylline concentration threshold. The elimination question keys multiple dose activated charcoal, and the dialysis criteria are not tested, so the concentration of 210 umol/L (about 38 mg/L) is below any extracorporeal threshold and is used only to show a rising sustained release exposure.
3. tox-39 keys parenteral cobalamin for nitrous oxide myeloneuropathy and uses Goldfrank for that question, since the Garakani review describes outcomes rather than a dose. A physician may prefer a neurology source for the treatment question.
