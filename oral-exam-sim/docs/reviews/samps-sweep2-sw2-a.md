## For the physician

- asthma-copd-24 and 38 carry cardiology and infectious disease content under KF4. That is the "comorbid conditions" key feature, and GINA is the source for the asthma-specific decisions. q2 of 38 (pMDI and spacer, not a nebulizer, with COVID-19) is labelled KF4 because the decision is driven by the coexisting infection. It could also be read as KF5.
- asthma-copd-24 q3: labetalol and sotalol are distractors on the basis that they also block beta2 receptors. That is standard pharmacology, not a GINA statement.
- asthma-copd-38 q3 follows GINA's advice to switch to an inhaled corticosteroid alone or to ICS-formoterol during nirmatrelvir-ritonavir. Only the first is offered.
- asthma-copd-41 and 42 rest on the BTS/ICS 2016 guideline, a UK source, because there is no Canadian guideline on NIV weaning or on ventilating a patient who cannot use a mask. 41 q3 relies on the stem giving a baseline PaCO2 of 46 mmHg. Recommendation 31 allows NIV to stop once pH and PaCO2 have normalized, which her PaCO2 of 55 has not.
- asthma-copd-42 q3 keys invasive ventilation through the stoma. No guideline addresses laryngectomy directly. The key rests on BTS/ICS recommendation 34 (intubate when no noninvasive interface can be used) and on the anatomy stated in the stem.
- asthma-copd-43 q1 uses KF2 for a work history finding. The official KF2 wording asks for a high-risk history that shapes management, and GINA calls the away-from-work question an essential screen. Please confirm this mapping.
- asthma-copd-45 q3 keys high-flow nasal therapy for an alert patient with pH 7.30 who refuses NIV and declines intubation. GOLD describes this use. The evidence is observational and from trials that allowed switching to NIV.
