## For the physician

- ischemic-heart-disease-13 departs from the direction. Please confirm primary PCI at a platelet count of 42 x 10^9/L. ESC 2023 supports an invasive strategy with expected survival of 6 months or more and gives platelet thresholds only for the antiplatelet drugs.
- infectious-diseases-02 does not credit or penalize tecovirimat. Evidence for it in clade II mpox is contested (STOMP was negative, and some guidance restricts it to advanced immunocompromise with expert input). Please say whether it should be credited in q1.
- infectious-diseases-02 q4 treats a partner who had 2 doses last year as not needing post-exposure vaccine, per the Canadian Immunization Guide wording ("have not received 2 doses"). Please confirm.
- msk-04 is a human trafficking story in British Columbia. The abuse 4 items (private interview, professional interpreter) also appear in abuse-domestic-13. The trafficking-specific items (documents, debt, freedom to leave, the hotline) are new. The admission key in q4 rests on one review (Kastanis 2024) recommending admission for multiple metacarpal fractures. Please confirm.

The high-specificity list in q2 credits scapular, spinous process and sternal fractures from wider AAP teaching, but the explanation names only the metaphyseal and rib fractures confirmed in the summary.
- tox-09 cites the Canadian Synthroid monograph as its main source. The monograph also lists antithyroid drugs, iodine and glucocorticoids as options, so none of these appear as distractors. Cholestyramine is supported only as a measure "used" after overdose, and no dose is keyed.
