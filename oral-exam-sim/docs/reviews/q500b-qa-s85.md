- q2 PASS. ESC 2015 major predictors verified on the open guideline page: fever over 38°C, subacute course, diastolic echo-free space over 20 mm, tamponade, no response to NSAIDs within 7 days. Key indices [2, 4, 7] map to the 24 mm effusion, 38.4°C and slow worsening over 10 days. No distractor is a predictor. Age 64 is not one.

The steroid reasoning (second line, low to moderate dose with colchicine, poor choice before TB is excluded) is correct. This key decision is common in the bank (NSAID with colchicine appears in many SAMPs), but here it is applied to a new context and is the literal key feature action, so it is acceptable.
