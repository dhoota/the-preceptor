## For the physician

- airway-02 rests on case reports and a small case series, because thermal epiglottitis in children has no guideline. Please confirm the operating room key (q2) in a tertiary centre with paediatric anesthesia and otolaryngology on site. One cited case was intubated in a burn centre resuscitation room instead.
- airway-09 q4 keys hemodialysis by the EXTRIP criteria. Urinary alkalinization is neither credited nor penalized. Please check that you agree.
- airway-09 q3 keys low cardiac output as the cause of a 24 mmHg end tidal to arterial gap. Her temperature of 35.0°C slightly alters the arterial value. The distractors avoid hypothermia for that reason.
- airway-10 q1 keys rocuronium over succinylcholine in a child, as the 2026 review prefers. Some paediatric emergency physicians still use succinylcholine. Please confirm this is a single best answer.
- airway-10 q3 keys a chest tube for a stable 2 cm pneumothorax in a ventilated child. Please confirm.
- airway-12 q3 atropine dose follows the ERC 2025 (0.5 mg, to 3 mg). Heart and Stroke teaching uses 1 mg. The write-in key credits either.
- analgesia-sedation-11 gives his lean body weight in the stem so candidates do not have to calculate it. Opioid dosing on lean body weight comes from an anesthesia review, not a burn guideline.
- Several sources are international or reviews because no current Canadian guideline covers these points. Five sources were checked from the abstract only (Maguire 2008, AACT 1999, Mactier 2014, Martyn 2006 and Ingrande 2010). The claims made from them are limited to what the abstracts state.
