## For the reviewers

- 48 q3: the joint statement lists the vacuum mattress as a transfer aid. The preference for it over a long board for a 90 minute flight rests on comfort and pressure injury, not on that statement alone.
- 49 q2: some teams would do the jaw thrust at the same moment as the tourniquet. The key relies on the xABCDE order in ATLS 11.
- 50 q3: the 300 mL/hour target comes from the review's summary of the literature. Some references give 200 to 300 mL/hour, so 200 was left out of the options.
- 51 q1, 51 q3 and 52 q1, 52 q3 rely on ATLS content that could not be read directly (intubation before transfer, oral gastric tube with a basal skull fracture, pediatric torso padding, nonoperative splenic care in children).
- 52 q4: the Orr study is a single-centre US cohort. The 15 minute wait was written in so the specialized team is clearly right.
