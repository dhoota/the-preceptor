## Description
Practise the oral exam the way it runs on the day.

Each case starts with a stem. The examiner asks timed questions. You answer out loud. Then you pick the option closest to what you said, and the case responds. Choose well and the case moves on. Miss something and the patient deteriorates, a consultant pushes back or a family member asks the hard question.

When the case ends, you see the model answer for every question. You mark yourself against the rubric, point by point. Critical points are flagged. Your score is broken down by domain.

What you get
- Original cases in the CCFP-EM oral style
- Branching examiner scripts that follow your decisions
- Timed questions, with an exam day mode that reads the examiner aloud
- Ask for vitals, exam findings and results at any point
- Model answers and a marking rubric for every case
- Weak area tracking across every case you run
- Spaced review of the points you missed, until they stick

Two cases are free. One purchase opens the full bank and every case added later. No subscription.

Private by design. No account. No tracking. Everything stays on your device. It works with no signal.
